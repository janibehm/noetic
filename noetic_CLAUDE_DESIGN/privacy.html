Privacy Policy

Last updated June 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information noetic collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. We designed our platform to collect as little as possible and to keep your creative work private.

1. Information we collect

Account information

When you create an account we collect your name, email and organization details, and billing information for paid plans.

Content you provide

We process the prompts, references and assets you submit in order to generate Outputs and operate the Services. These are stored on your behalf and associated with your account.

Usage data

We collect technical information such as device, browser, and feature usage to keep the platform fast and reliable.

2. How we use information

  • To provide, secure and improve the Services;
  • To process payments and manage your account;
  • To communicate updates, with your consent for marketing;
  • To detect abuse and enforce our terms.

We do not use your Inputs or Outputs to train our base models, and we never sell your personal information.

3. Cookies

We use essential cookies to run the platform and, with your consent, analytics and marketing cookies. You can manage your choices at any time through Cookie Settings.

4. Sharing

We share information only with service providers who help us operate the platform under strict confidentiality, or where required by law. Enterprise data residency options are available.

5. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, export or delete your personal information. To exercise these rights, email privacy@noetic.ai.

6. Retention & security

We retain information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Services, and we protect it with encryption in transit and at rest, access controls and ongoing monitoring.

7. Contact

Questions about privacy? Reach our team at privacy@noetic.ai.
