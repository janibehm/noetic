If you believe you've found a security vulnerability in noetic, we'd like to hear from you and will work with you to resolve it quickly. This page explains our scope, safe-harbor commitment and how to report.

In scope

  • noetic.ai and its subdomains
  • The noetic web application and dashboard
  • The public API at api.noetic.ai and official SDKs

Out of scope

  • Volumetric attacks (DoS/DDoS) and social engineering
  • Reports from automated scanners without a working proof of concept
  • Issues in third-party services we don't control

Safe harbor

We will not pursue legal action for good-faith security research that follows this policy: respect user privacy, avoid degrading our services, and don't access or modify data that isn't yours. Give us reasonable time to remediate before any public disclosure.

What to include

A clear description of the issue, the affected endpoint or component, reproduction steps, and the potential impact. Proof-of-concept code or screenshots help us triage faster.
